Scope: source, docs, constitution, roadmap, evidence JSON, and recent commits, read on 2026-11-26 at commit `b502412`. Purpose: separate what is implemented, what is experimentally validated, and what is unproven, and check that the latest conclusions follow from the raw evidence. Nothing here repeats an experiment; the one number computed anew is the seed interval of the N2 pilot, from the committed raw JSON.

## N2 re-check against the raw JSON

`novelty-n2-evidence/dev-1e6.json`, 3 seeds, paired differences F (compiled init) over E (random tables, same architecture), BPB: fiction 0.0014, 0.0050, 0.0256; non-fiction 0.0096, 0.0346, -0.0062. Mean 0.0107 / 0.0127, sd 0.0130 / 0.0205, 95% t-interval half-widths 0.032 / 0.051, Cohen d_z 0.82 / 0.62. **Every 95% interval includes 0**; the decision record's reading ("positive on 5 of 6 cells, within seed spread, inconclusive") is supported, and so is "do not scale". F over D (training the compiled circuit rather than freezing it): non-fiction seeds 0.0017, 0.0417, -0.0274. The strongest N2 fact is architectural: D (nothing trained in the circuit) beats the plain GRU by 0.03 / 0.06 BPB.

## Findings that bound later claims

1. **Seeds vary only the GRU initialization.** In N0-N2 the exposure stream and the held-out text are fixed, so seed spread omits data variance. GDM varies the exposure window per seed.
2. **The "20,000-parameter GRU" is the recurrent cell budget.** The total with embedding and readout is 47,594 parameters (A) and 55,863 (circuit arms). Parameter matching in N2 was done on totals; GDM does the same and reports effective (updatable) counts.
3. **The N2 circuit is a 256-byte softmax attention window**, O(window) state per step, not a bounded fast-weight state. A result about it says little about a gated-delta memory.
4. **Mixer learning rate sat on the grid edge in N1 and N2**, so G/H/F hybrids were slightly under-tuned. GDM widens the grid to 5 values.
5. **Hybrid mixers adapt on the held-out stream prequentially** (scored before updated). That is legal online learning, not leakage of weights, but it means "with Kneser-Ney" numbers include adaptation on test text. GDM reports the unmixed BPB as primary.
6. **Hyperparameter selection used a separate probe role** (no leakage found). Budgets per arm were equal in N2 (6 configurations for F and E, none for A). GDM gives every arm a 4-configuration grid.
7. **No evidence of evaluation leakage was found** in the code paths read (held-out streams are separate roles, whole-document splits, 10-gram contamination check against exposure; evaluation functions do not update weights). The GDM harness asserts the weight hash is unchanged across every evaluation and probe.
8. **Constitution invariants 10-13 are not met by the research arms** (host-side loops). GDM is the same kind of research arm: it supports a scientific claim about a mechanism, not a mainline-model claim.
9. **The wording "Phase 0" in the request**: the repository has no gate called Phase 0; milestone names are used. No milestone is declared complete here.

## What the audit concludes for the next experiment

The decision record leaves three options. This work takes (a) (a second compiled family, the gated-delta memory) and (b) (measure retention and novel-context behaviour directly), together, because they are the same experiment with different metrics, and does not scale N2. Reason it is not a repeat: a different mechanism (bounded delta-rule state, not windowed attention), and metrics N2 never measured (retention after unrelated input, forgetting under a domain shift, restart behaviour).
