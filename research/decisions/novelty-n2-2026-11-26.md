# Experiment N2: the trainable compiled circuit (pilot) and the scaling decision

Status: **INCONCLUSIVE for H0 at the pilot; H2 not supported by the pilot; do not scale to 10^7 with this circuit. No constitution change requested.**

## What was run
`zerolm-circuit`, development role, 10^6 exposure bytes, 100,000 held-out bytes per role, GRU seeds 9201-9203, hidden role unread. Evidence: `novelty-n2-evidence/dev-1e6.json`. Arms A, A+ (parameter-matched), D (compiled circuit frozen), F (compiled initial value, trained), E (same architecture, random tables; this is also the scrambled-weights control X1). Init scalars and the circuit learning-rate multiplier were chosen per arm from one shared grid on a probe slice; the mixer rate came from the same probe and landed on the grid edge (0.3).

## Results (mean over seeds; BPB, fiction / non-fiction)
Alone: A 2.744 / 3.035, A+ 2.716 / 3.020, D 2.718 / 2.976, F 2.706 / 2.971, E 2.717 / 2.983. Paired gains: F over E 0.011 / 0.013; F over A+ 0.010 / 0.049; F over D 0.012 / 0.005; in the hybrids F over G 0.008 / 0.012, over H 0.005 / 0.008, with a random circuit (E) already giving 0.004 / 0.008 over G. Bytes for F to reach A's final BPB: 640-810 kB; for E 780-820 kB; E's own final BPB is reached by F at 850-980 kB (ratio to E about 0.85-0.98).

## Reading against the pre-registered rules
- H2 (compiled init beats random init of the same architecture): the direction is positive on 5 of 6 seed-by-role cells, but the size (about 0.01 BPB, a P1 bytes ratio of about 0.91) is within seed spread (sd 0.012 over 3 seeds); no interval was computed that excludes 0 across seeds.
- H3 (the gain is from the algorithm): E is the X1 control and reproduces about half of the circuit's gain inside the hybrid; most of the gain over a plain GRU is architecture (D over A is 0.03 / 0.06 with nothing trained).
- H4 (trainable beats frozen): F over D is 0.012 / 0.005, inconsistent in sign for non-fiction seeds.
- Protected metrics: not yet measured (kappa, throughput ratio); GRU-side cost of the circuit arms was about 1.7x training wall time.
- Outcome rule: F beats A and (weakly) E, but the hybrid result is small and X1 reproduces part of it. This sits between ENGINEERING SUCCESS ONLY and INCONCLUSIVE; with 3 seeds and a pilot-only role it is recorded as **INCONCLUSIVE**, leaning toward ENGINEERING SUCCESS ONLY.

## Frozen from the pilot (before any hidden-role read)
`model/specs/novelty.json` now sets: `delta_floor_bytes_ratio` 0.8 (F must reach E's BPB with at most 80% of E's bytes; the pilot's 0.91 does not meet it), `delta_floor_novel_bpb` 0.02 (twice the pilot F-over-E gain; at least the pilot seed sd), `seeds` 5 (pilot sd 0.012 against an effect of 0.02). These were set from pilot seeds 9201-9203 only; the confirmatory run must use new seeds.

## Decision
1. Per the scaling rule (`docs/experimental-design.md`, budgets), **do not run 10^7 or Gate B for this circuit**: no advantage over random init has been shown at 10^6, and no testable reason exists for it to emerge only at scale. The circuit stays as an architecture result (D over A), which is engineering, not novelty.
2. The only compiled prior so far is the match circuit, whose headroom over count-plus-match is about 0.03 BPB (N1). A decisive test needs a prior with more headroom or a different metric. Candidates, for the project lead to choose among (none changes the constitution): (a) a second compiled family with larger room on novel-context metrics (copy-with-transform / induction over learned representations, compiled gated-delta memory); (b) measure H5 (retention under distribution shift) and P2 (novel-context BPB) directly, since BPB room is exhausted by H; (c) stop the novelty track and record ENGINEERING SUCCESS ONLY.
3. Before any confirmatory run: widen the mixer-rate grid, compute the seed-paired bootstrap intervals the rule requires, add equal-compute arms X2, and put the circuit on the neural ISA with reference-interpreter parity (invariants 10-13); a host-side circuit cannot support a mainline claim.
