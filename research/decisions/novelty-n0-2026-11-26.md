## Experiment N0
Does a match-model prior (the simplest compiled algorithmic prior) have headroom over Kneser-Ney 5 / PPM-C 5? Development role only; hidden role unread. Results (`novelty-n0-evidence/`, SHA-256 `69cc3ca0...e648` v1, `96aec7fc...3a53` v2): with the v2 mixer, gain over KN-5 on fiction 0.120 / 0.044 / 0.011 BPB at 10^5 / 10^6 / 10^7 exposure bytes, on non-fiction 0.355 / 0.227 / 0.148 (95% intervals exclude 0 except exposure+in-context fiction at 10^7: 0.0001 [-0.0056, 0.0061]). The v1 mixer lost on fiction at 10^6 and 10^7 (-0.035, -0.087); v2 was written after seeing that, on the same role, so v2 numbers are pilot figures, not confirmatory.

## Decision
1. BPB cannot be the primary metric for the induction prior: its ceiling is small beside the 0.6 BPB gap to KN-5, and a conventional hybrid (arm H) captures it. This does not reject H0.
2. The central comparison (trainable compiled circuit against random init of the same architecture, arms D/E/F/X1) is not yet runnable. Required next: arms G and H (N1) as the bar, then the trainable circuit (N2). Nothing is claimed.
3. Roadmap: add Gate N inside `scalable-core` before the 10^8-byte run (`docs/roadmap.md` section 5, Phase 10 note). Gate B's own requirements are unchanged.
