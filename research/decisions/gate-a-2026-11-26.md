## Which row of the decision table applies

"P1 fails while A3 shows clear headroom: the central hypothesis is not supported at this scale." The options named by the roadmap:

- **(a)** amend constitution invariant 4 to allow single-pass online gradient updates in the mainline (still no offline pretraining, every update logged; the NNCP route), then rerun this gate with A3's rule as the mainline arm.
- **(b)** narrow the objective to a compressor-grade system (Level L1) with compiled circuits and readout learning, and drop the language-model milestones.
- **(c)** discontinue the track.

## Evidence that bears on the choice

For (a): the control gets a 1.37 BPB probe gain with the same architecture, initialization, and bytes, so the architecture can learn useful features from this stream; the gain is retained across a domain shift ($\kappa \approx 1.0$); and the CONTROL's calibration is much better (ECE 0.09 against 0.25).

Against stopping at (a) alone: even the CONTROL reaches only 2.91 held-out BPB at 20,000 cell parameters and 10^7 bytes, against 2.18 for Kneser-Ney order 5. It does not reach Level L1 here. Whether a larger core or more bytes closes that gap is what Phase 10 and 11 would measure; the pilot saw 1.3 BPB probe gain at 200,000 parameters on 10^6 bytes.

Limits of the negative result (what the gate did not test): only one local rule (e-prop with fixed random feedback, diagonal approximation) was run; the optional second, error-free predictive rule was never pre-registered or run. Its learning rate was searched over six values on two pilot seeds. The core was 20,000 parameters (the larger core was piloted only with the version-1 learning rates, all of which diverged). One corpus family at 10^7 bytes. One version-1 pilot run at 200,000 parameters had a positive probe gain (0.21) on one seed while its own readout diverged; this is one run on one seed and is recorded, not used.

## Recommendation (the lead decides)

Option (a), by the roadmap's own table: the evidence matches its row, it keeps the "no offline pretraining" and "every update logged" properties that define the project, and it makes the next gate (rerun with A3's rule as the mainline arm) a direct test of whether single-pass online gradient learning gets from the CONTROL's 2.9 BPB toward the 2.2 BPB of the count baselines. This amends invariant 4 and so needs the lead's explicit approval.

Before (a), the lead may prefer to spend one pre-registered attempt on the second local rule, since the roadmap allows at most one more rule, to be registered before any run. A new spec version would carry it, and the failure of e-prop would stay on record in the spec's `history`. This record does not recommend it over (a): e-prop captured 0.7% of the gain, and the predictive rule has no stronger prior.

Options (b) and (c) are not supported by the evidence yet: backpropagation shows headroom, and nothing has shown that a compressor-grade system is the best this architecture can do.

## What stays unchanged until the lead decides

Phase 10 does not start. The milestone `representation-feasibility` stays `in_progress`: it also needs the CI evidence upload (`representation-gate` job) and `real-text` is not complete.
